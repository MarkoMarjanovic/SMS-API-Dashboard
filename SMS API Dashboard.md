Summary

We’re looking for an experienced SMS messaging specialist to help select and set up an API-based messaging solution for reaching our 100,000+ existing paid clients.



We’re open to Twilio or another reliable provider. Our target is under $0.01 USD per outbound, single-segment SMS, including provider and carrier fees. Please verify whether this is achievable for our recipient countries and sending volume.



The work includes:



Comparing providers and recommending the most cost-effective solution.



Setting up the account, API integration, sending numbers, and required sender registrations.



Connecting an easy-to-use dashboard for importing contacts, personalizing messages, scheduling campaigns, and managing replies.



Reviewing consent records and configuring opt-out handling and suppression lists.



Setting up delivery reports, error handling, sending limits, and spending controls.



Testing the integration with a small campaign before expanding.



Providing documentation and a walkthrough so our team can operate the system.



We want someone with proven experience implementing high-volume SMS systems, managing deliverability, and explaining the complete cost of sending campaigns. Accounts, credentials, and any custom code must remain under our ownership.



Please include the following in your proposal:



Which provider would you recommend, and why?



Can you achieve our under-1-cent target, including carrier fees? Include pricing evidence, destination assumptions, and any volume commitments.



What would sending 100,000 single-segment messages cost? Show messaging charges, setup fees, registration fees, and recurring costs separately.



Which dashboard or sending interface would you use?



What similar projects have you completed?



What is your setup fee, estimated timeline, and optional ongoing support rate?



Please explain how longer messages or special characters affect billing. If our pricing target is not achievable, provide the lowest realistic rate and explain the assumptions.



We’re seeking someone who can help us choose the service, complete the setup, and deliver a working system our team can use confidently.
